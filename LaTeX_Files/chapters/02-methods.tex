Image processing is a highly relevant procedure for deploying statistical pipelines. Without a solid and robust preprocessing the downstream task will not produce any relevant information. For this project we gathered pre-procecced sMRI images.
The fully description of the pre-processing steps can be investigated in J. Menzes et al. \cite{MENEZES2026111143}. Here we are going to explain the pre-processing in a short version.
 
One of the methods of preprocessing in MRI images is the bias field correction. The bias fields is a 
low-frequency spatially varying MRI artifact resulting from spatial inhomogeneity of the magnetic field, variations in the sensitivity of the reception coil, and the interaction between the magnetic field and the human body.
The bias fiels is dependent on the strength of the magnetic filed. By increasing the magnetic field, which increases the spatial resolution also increases the bias field, resulting in a higher Signal to noise ration.
